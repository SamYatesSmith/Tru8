## Launch blockers (must fix before brand push)
- Hard-coded GBP prices ship against the active price-gate lock (project_pricing_not_set_2026_06_23): /pricing shows £20/mo, £200/yr, From £75/mo plus a price in the meta description, and /developers hard-codes GBP tiers. The lock says no display price on ANY pricing surface until the founder sets one, and currency is locked-open ($ decision conflicts with £). Gate these qualitatively and strip the price from meta until the founder signs off — do not invent or alter numbers.
- Compliance/regulatory framing in a linked blog post violates the honest-framing positioning lock (no compliance/regulatory claim). Remove or recast as a concrete user action (publish/re-check/escalate) before launch.
- Forbidden profession names on the two front doors (/ console preview, /research carousel) violate the action-names language lock. Relabel to Evidence/Sources/Timeline/Gaps/Map/Video; profession allowed only as a muted subtitle.
- Forbidden user-facing word 'policy' in the shared legal-layout footer ('Questions about this policy?'), which also self-links nonsensically on /contact. Fix in LegalPageLayout (affects all legal/support pages).
- Tagline locale inconsistency on the core brand line: US 'organize' on /,/pricing vs UK 'organise/organises' on /compare,/about,/developers. The signature line must read consistently (US on marketing) before the brand goes out — trivial sweep, but brand-defining.

## Design consistency issues
- The shipped Stitch document-grammar system (numbered SheetHeaders, mono left spine, 2px orange top rule, rotated registration glyph) is fully applied only on / and /pricing. Every other surface diverges: /developers, /about and /compare run a bespoke 'Module —' eyebrow system; /contact (and the shared LegalPageLayout) uses the legacy rounded-card 'legal' layout; /blog and the blog posts read as a generic prose/list template. The result is that the brand looks like several different products.
- font-bold headings appear across /developers, /about, /compare, /blog and the blog posts, breaking the system's 'size is the hierarchy lever, weight is reserved' rule used on the hero/record sheets.
- Orange is used as a FILL rather than a stroke/mark on multiple pages — /developers (primary CTA + badges + 4px left rules + filled step numbers), /about (text-accent tagline + 4px orange left border), /contact (lone orange MapPin) — against the single-accent stroke discipline kept on / and /pricing.
- Orphan/incoherent sheet numbers: /research ships a lone '04 / COMPARE' header with no 01-03 (copy-paste leak from the homepage component, literally commented 'Homepage — Sheet 04'); /pricing ships a lone '05' on a standalone page; the homepage FAQ breaks the numbered series by having no SheetHeader at all.
- Console preview on / exposes a raw bracketed internal route '/dashboard/check/[id]?view=librarian' and profession-named view labels, reading as unfinished/leaked internal detail on the page that sells precision.
- Imported carousel on /research breaks the font hierarchy (font-light + italic) and skips a heading level (h4 cards under h2).
- zinc-300/zinc-400 low-contrast text traps recur on essentially every page (mono eyebrows, refs, back links, footnotes, the /compare capability-table 'No'/'Unverified' marks, the /developers white-on-orange CTA) — a systemic AA contrast problem, not a per-page one.

## IA / nav issues
- Researcher stranded on developer pages: neither /developers nor /compare body links to the human /research console, so a journalist who lands there has no path to their own workflow (nav exposes 'Research App' but the page bodies never do).
- /about is a funnel dead-end — both CTAs and the only links dump back to the homepage, with no link to the research app or any sample/proof record; weak internal-link equity during the active visibility push.
- /blog does not surface /research and its meta description is developer-skewed, so the discovery surface built for SEO steers researchers away both on-page and in the SERP snippet.
- /pricing Console (priced) and Free CTAs point to the identical destination — there is no actual path to choose or pay for Console, so a displayed price has no checkout behind it.
- JSON-LD gaps and mis-typing: / is typed as DeveloperApplication (reinforces the dev frame to search/AI engines); /about has no AboutPage/Organization, /contact no ContactPoint/Organization, /blog no Blog/ItemList — inconsistent with the schema hygiene already on /compare and /developers.
- OG images missing/generic for /research and /blog (defer the image itself to the open I-06 visual review, but the metadata gap is real).
- /developers primary 'Documentation' links fall back to http://localhost:8000 if NEXT_PUBLIC_API_URL is unset in the production build — confirm the env var or fall back to the real public docs origin so the links can't ship dead.
- /pricing and /about have heading-order/structure issues that also hurt SEO: /pricing has NO h1 (starts at h2) on the most commercially important page; /contact skips h1→h3.
