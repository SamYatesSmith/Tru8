# Prompt: Full Report Card

## Design Request

Design a social media Open Graph card for sharing a complete fact verification report.

---

## Specifications

- **Dimensions:** 1200 x 630 px
- **Safe zone:** Keep text within 1100 x 580 px (50px margins)
- **Must be readable at 500px width** (mobile rendering)

---

## Brand

**Product:** Tru8 - Evidence verification platform (NOT a fact-checker - we aggregate what sources say)

**Aesthetic:** Professional, data-forward, authoritative. Think Bloomberg terminal meets scientific journal. Clean, modern, trustworthy.

**Typography:** Inter or similar clean sans-serif. Bold headings, clear hierarchy.

---

## Content Layout (Top to Bottom)

### 1. Header Bar
- Tru8 logo or wordmark (left)
- "EVIDENCE REPORT" label/badge (right or beside logo)

### 2. Title Zone
- Article title in bold, 2 lines maximum
- Example: **"Nipah Virus Outbreak in Kerala, India"**

### 3. Stats Row (The Hook)
Three key metrics with icons:
- **8 claims** examined
- **231 sources** searched
- **47 evidence** pieces cited

### 4. Source Quality Breakdown
Visual representation of source tiers (horizontal bars, segments, or badges):
- Health Authorities: 12
- Scientific Journals: 8
- Tier 1 News: 15
- Government Sources: 5
- Other Credible: 7

This demonstrates the BREADTH and QUALITY of research.

### 5. Source Names (Social Proof)
List top credible sources:
**WHO • CDC • The Lancet • BBC • Reuters • Nature**

### 6. Footer CTA
- "See what the sources say →"
- tru8.app

## Variations Needed

1. **Dark theme** - Dark background (#0f1419), light text
2. **Light theme** - White/light background, dark text

---

## Visual Direction

**Do:**
- Bold contrast for social feed visibility
- Clear visual hierarchy: Title → Stats → Sources → CTA
- Use whitespace - don't crowd elements
- Make stats visually prominent (large numbers)
- Source tier bars should be scannable at a glance

**Don't:**
- Too much text - this is a hook, not the full report
- Cluttered layout - every element must earn its place
- Generic imagery - the DATA is the visual
- Low contrast text
- Stock photos or illustrations
